ML Papers of the Day

A short video series explaining notable new AI and machine-learning research papers from arXiv, accurately and in plain language.

Each episode is produced by an automated pipeline: it selects papers, extracts their findings, writes and narrates a script, and renders the video. Every number shown or spoken is checked against the paper before an episode is published.

This application signs in to the channel owner's own YouTube account to upload finished episodes. It is not offered to other users.
